Fear & Hunger Co-op is out: Termina and Fear & Hunger 1 for up to 4 players

Friends, the mod is ready! You can now play Fear & Hunger 2: Termina and Fear & Hunger 1 with two, three or four players.

How to play
• On one PC - keyboard and gamepads, the screen splits when you walk apart.
• Online - everyone on their own PC, over the local network or the internet.

What's inside
• Everyone has their own character, their own menu, their own turns in battle and their own torch.
• Fight together or each in your own battle - run up to a friend's fight and join in.
• Death that matters: your body stays on the map, and a soul stone can bring you back.
• The protagonist died? The next player takes over and the story goes on.
• PvP: cut off a friend's arm, leg or head, fight to the death, kill the protagonist and become the protagonist yourself.
• Bring your own character from your save, plenty of settings and cheats, English and Russian.

And in Termina - new scenes and two new endings. You can save the other contestants of the festival, climb the tower together and decide with Per'kele who joins the Sulfur God and who breaks the circle. No more details - let it be a surprise.

Installation: unpack the archive into the game folder - that's it.
