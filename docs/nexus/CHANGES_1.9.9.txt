Fear & Hunger Co-op v1.9.9 - what's new

* A character a player plays is no longer treated as a companion who leaves. In Termina Marina left the party every morning and the message came at every change of map.
* Termina: what happens "to the protagonist" in an event a player started happens to that player's character. A bear trap now takes the legs of whoever stepped on it.
* Termina, Hexen: each player's character learns from their own branch; skills they already know show as learned, new ones show their stone at once; the soul stones counter updates again.
* Termina: a player's character who knows a craft (Weaponcraft, Trapcraft, Explosives, Botanism, Toxicology) brings its schematics, so crafting works for them too.
* A player's character's moonscorched form and companion scenes are left out, as if they were the protagonist (setting "Played characters' NPCs"). The evil doubles stay.
* Online: automatic events that follow a player's own event (the lockers of tunnel 7) stay that player's own.
* Online: an item used from a player's menu waits until they close the menu; it no longer stopped everybody while somebody was in a menu.
* Online: starting the game in the dream tower after loading a save no longer replays the wake-up scene and moves time on a second time.

Update: unpack the new version over the old one. Online, every player needs the same version.
