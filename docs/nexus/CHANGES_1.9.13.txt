Fear & Hunger Co-op v1.9.13 - what's new

* New cheat: phase walk (on / off) - every player walks through walls, to get out when somebody gets stuck in one.
* F6 / F7 (quick save / quick load) are installed by default, and they work with the game in Russian too (they never did). When they can't be used, a message says why.
* Fixed (online): a story scene that starts by itself after a player's step (Caligura attacking Levi) is for everybody again - each player had their own talk and he was killed twice.
* Termina: only whoever climbs a ladder goes at ladder speed; the others no longer slow down.
* coop_memory.log also shows where the time goes each minute (game logic, drawing, map refreshes, events on the map), to find the slowdowns on big maps.

Update: run install.bat of the new version. Online, every player needs the same version.
